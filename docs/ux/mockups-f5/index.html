OpenGlass — F5 design mockups (10 variants)

Static HTML, shadcn-style semantic tokens. Data shapes follow the backend contract (User, ChatSummary, Message, GroupMember). Mockups only — not wired to the app.

01 · Zinc & IndigoClassic shadcn neutral + one confident indigo. Safest, most familiar messenger look.
02 · Navy ExecutiveDeep navy header band, cool grey canvas, teal for positive states. Corporate and calm.
03 · Graphite NightDark-first graphite with emerald. Reads as a security tool; private layer goes pure black.
04 · Warm StoneWarm off-white paper, burnt-orange accent, squircle avatars. Friendly but still restrained.
05 · Cobalt FlatHard edges, 1px borders, cobalt blue, dense rows. Closest to a Bloomberg/IDE feel — pure business.
06 · Sage TrustMuted forest green on pale sage — Signal-adjacent trust cues, soft and readable.
07 · Slate TabsBottom tab bar (Chats / Contacts / Settings), bubble tails, slate-blue accent. Most 'native app' structure.
08 · Desktop SplitResponsive desktop layout: list | chat | info panel. Same tokens, wide breakpoint (≥1024px).
09 · Frosted GlassOn-brand 'OpenGlass': translucent blurred bars over a soft gradient, violet-blue accent.
10 · Mono + Violet LayersPublic = strict black & white. Violet appears ONLY where encryption is active, so layer = colour.
